About the condition

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing teeth that have been damaged by decay, wear, trauma, or previous dental work. The goal is to restore both function and appearance, so your teeth look good and work as they should for the long term.

Failing restorations, cracked teeth, worn enamel, and cavities are all common conditions that restorative dentistry addresses. Left untreated, these problems typically worsen over time, leading to more complex and costly treatment down the line. Early intervention is nearly always the better option.

  • Composite fillings matched precisely to your natural tooth colour
  • Ceramic crowns to protect and restore heavily damaged or root-treated teeth
  • Inlays and onlays as conservative alternatives to full crowns
  • Bridges to replace one or more missing teeth supported by adjacent teeth
  • Full-mouth rehabilitation for patients with extensive wear or multiple failing restorations

Restorative treatment at Caliora is planned comprehensively. We assess the whole mouth, not just the presenting problem, and develop a phased treatment plan that prioritises clinical need while fitting within your timeline and budget.
